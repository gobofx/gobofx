# Flip

A card with `from` on the front and `to` on the back turns over in 3D. The
area around the card is transparent while it turns.

- **Axis** — turn about the vertical or the horizontal axis.
- **Perspective** — foreshortening strength.
- **Ease** — timing.

## Use it when

- Before and after, two sides of a story, a comparison.
- Kids' and game content, where the card turning is the delight.
- A graphic card that literally has a back.

## Avoid when

- Narrative. A spinning card is a show device.
- Two live-action shots. The card edges reveal whatever is behind the
  layer while it turns.
- More than once per project. Otherwise, hard cut.
