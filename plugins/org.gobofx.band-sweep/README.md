# Band Sweep

A graphic band sweeps across the lower part of the frame and drags the cut
behind it: `to` arrives as a slanted wipe riding on the band's trailing
edge, so the new shot is in place the moment the band has passed.

- **Direction** — the band travels left to right or right to left.
- **Height** — band height, as a fraction of the frame.
- **Offset** — gap between the band and the bottom edge; 0 sits it on the edge.
- **Width** — band length, as a fraction of the frame width.
- **Slant** — tilt of the band ends and of the wipe edge, in degrees.
- **Color** — the band's fill (alpha respected).
- **Softness** — feather of every edge.
- **Ease** — timing.

## Use it when

- Lower-third territory: news, sport, corporate and event cuts with a graphic
  identity.
- The band colour can carry the brand; the shot change then feels designed.
- Listicles and chaptered content, where each cut is a new heading.

## Avoid when

- The subject lives in the lower part of the frame; the band hides it twice.
- Cinematic material; it is television grammar and will look like it.
- You want the chevron version: Arrows points the way.
