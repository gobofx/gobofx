# Clones

`from` multiplies into a grid of copies of itself, then the copies shrink
away one by one to leave `to`. The grid grows continuously from the single
frame, so there is no pop at the start.

- **Count** — copies across and down at the midpoint (2–8).
- **Gap** — gutter between the copies, as a fraction of a cell; `to` shows through it.
- **Ease** — timing.

## Use it when

- Montage or promo cutting where the outgoing shot deserves a flourish.
- Music-driven edits: hit the multiply on the downbeat, the shrink on the next.
- Product and feature reels where the repeated image reads as "many".

## Avoid when

- Narrative scenes; the multiplication breaks the fiction completely.
- Shots with faces close up: nine tiny faces is unsettling, not stylish.
- You need the same gesture with motion in it: Clone Spin spins the copies.
