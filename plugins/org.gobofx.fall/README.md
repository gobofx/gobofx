# Fall

`from` tips over backwards around its bottom edge and falls away from the
camera, darkening as it turns, until `to` is all that is left. A door
falling off its hinges, seen from the front.

- **Perspective** — 0 nearly flat, 1 strong foreshortening.
- **Shade** — how dark `from` gets as it turns away from the light.
- **Ease** — timing (the fall accelerates on its own).

## Use it when

- A chapter, scene or level ends with finality: the old picture literally
  drops out of the way.
- Game, product and app sequences where screens are physical panels.
- Pairing with Drop In: one shot falls away, the next drops in.

## Avoid when

- Soft, continuous storytelling; it is abrupt and a little theatrical.
- `to` is dark or empty: the fall reveals nothing and reads as a glitch.
- Vertical video, where the hinge sits far from the eye line and the
  foreshortening looks wrong.
