# Mirror

Reflect one side of the picture onto the other across a vertical or a
horizontal axis, or both. The default folds the left half over the right.
Where an off-centre axis runs out of picture the frame is transparent.

- **Axis** — vertical (left / right), horizontal (top / bottom), or both (a quadrant).
- **Position** — where the axis sits, 0..1 across the frame.
- **Keep** — which side is the original: left or top, or right or bottom.

## Use it when

- A symmetrical frame: a face, a building, a landscape over water, a title
  centred on the fold.
- A kaleidoscopic or a dreamlike beat in a music cut, slid across the frame
  by keyframing Position.
- Hiding something at one edge of a static shot by reflecting the clean
  side over it.

## Avoid when

- The shot needs to be flipped, not folded: use Flop.
- Faces are near the fold; a face split by its own reflection is unsettling
  and reads as an error unless that is the point.
- The reflection would show a crew, a slate or a logo twice.
