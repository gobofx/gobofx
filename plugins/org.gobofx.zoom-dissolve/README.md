# Zoom Dissolve

A radial zoom blur builds through the cut: `from` streaks outward from the
centre as it fades, `to` arrives streaking inward.

- **Amount** — streak length; **Center** — the zoom origin.
- **Ease** — timing.

## Use it when

- Music video and trailer cuts that want a rush through the join.
- A time jump with energy: the same subject, a different day.
- A travel sequence between locations, **Center** on the horizon.

## Avoid when

- Drama. The quiet version is Blur Dissolve; the honest one is Cross
  Dissolve.
- Back to back. Two in a row and the audience is seasick.
- **Center** not on the subject in both shots. The streaks then point at
  nothing.
