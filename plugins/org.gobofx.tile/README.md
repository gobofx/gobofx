# Tile

The picture repeated on a grid: a wall of the same frame, mirrored so the
seams vanish, or shifted row by row like brickwork. 1×1 is a no-op.

- **Columns / Rows** — how many copies across and down (1 to 12 each).
- **Mirror** — flip every other tile so edges meet their own reflection.
- **Row Offset** — slide odd rows by a fraction of a tile, -0.5 to 0.5; 0.5 is a brick bond.
- **Gap** — transparent gutter between the tiles (and half of one along the frame edge), in pixels; whatever is below shows through.

## Use it when

- A multi-screen, video-wall or contact-sheet moment should show the same
  shot many times at once.
- A pattern or texture clip should fill the frame seamlessly: turn Mirror on
  and let the reflections hide the edges.
- A title card or background needs a repeating motif built from a single
  graphic.

## Avoid when

- The intent is a mirror fold, not a repeat: Kaleidoscope folds around a
  point, this only tiles.
- The shot has faces or a clear subject and the brief is naturalistic;
  repetition reads as a gag or a music-video device.
- You need each copy to differ in time or colour: this is one frame
  repeated, nothing more.
