# Invert

A negative of the picture, all channels or one, or brightness only.

- **Amount** — blend between the original and the negative.
- **Channels** — rgb, a single channel, or luma (inverts brightness, keeps hue).

## Use it when

- A negative hit in a music video or horror beat, one or two frames.
- Inverting a matte: **Channels** on luma turns a key inside out.
- Checking a graphic or an alpha. Problems hide in black and show in white.

## Avoid when

- Partial **Amount** on footage. Halfway to negative is grey mush.
- Skin, unless the point is horror. Inverted faces are cyan.
- You want a colour shift rather than a negative. Use Hue/Saturation.
