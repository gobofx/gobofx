# Blur Dissolve

`from` blurs away while `to` blurs in, crossfading through the softest point.

- **Radius** — the peak blur in pixels.
- **Ease** — timing.

## Use it when

- Softening a jump in time with a dreamy edge: waking, memory, a fever.
- Hiding a framing mismatch between two shots of the same subject. The blur
  swallows the misalignment a Cross Dissolve would show.
- Interview to B-roll and back, when the cut should feel like drifting
  attention rather than a decision.

## Avoid when

- The cut needs to land on a beat or feel decisive. A hard cut does that; a
  blur delays it.
- Both shots are already soft or dark. The peak reads as a focus fault.
- Plain "time passes" with a clean look. Cross Dissolve says it without the
  smear.
