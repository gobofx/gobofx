# Billboard

The frame is a row of vertical slats with `from` on one face and `to` on
the other, like a rotating roadside sign. Each slat turns about its own
axis, the turn running left to right across the frame; the gaps behind the
turning slats are transparent.

- **Count** — slats across the frame (2–24).
- **Stagger** — 0 all slats turn together, 1 a wave from the left edge.
- **Perspective** — foreshortening of a turning slat.
- **Ease** — timing.

## Use it when

- Advertising, retail and promo material; the sign metaphor is the message.
- Cutting between posters, packshots or stills, which suit the flat faces.
- A bumper needs a mechanical, rhythmic beat; set Stagger high.

## Avoid when

- Footage with strong horizontal motion: the slats shear it as they turn.
- Quiet scenes; the sign is a loud, commercial gesture.
- You want one turning card rather than a row: Flip does that.
