# Poke

A finger pushed into the picture: inside the radius the pixels spread out
from the point, sharpest at the centre and fading to nothing at the rim,
with no seam. A negative Amount pulls them in instead. At Amount 0 the
picture passes through unchanged. Pixels pulled from outside the frame
repeat the edge.

- **Center** — where the finger lands.
- **Radius** — how far the dent reaches, in frame heights.
- **Amount** — 1 a full push out, -1 a full pull in; 0 is a no-op.

## Use it when

- A touch, a press or an impact on a surface: keyframe Amount in and out
  over a few frames on the beat.
- A rubber, a jelly or a screen being prodded in a product or an
  explainer shot.
- A quick, local emphasis on a graphic — a button, a logo — that should feel
  physical rather than scaled.

## Avoid when

- A smooth magnifying glass is what you mean: Bulge's dome is rounder and
  reads as a lens, this one reads as a dent.
- Faces, unless the poke is the joke; the point of the profile lands on a
  nose or an eye before anything else.
- Text inside the radius; the sharp centre breaks letterforms.
