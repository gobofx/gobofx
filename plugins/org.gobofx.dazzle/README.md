# Dazzle

Star-shaped glints on the highlights: everything above the threshold throws
an arm out along each point of a star, the way a bright light flares through
a lens iris or a star filter. Intensity 0 is a no-op.

- **Threshold** — brightness that glints; at 1 only pure white does.
- **Points** — arms on the star, 4 to 8; one always points straight up.
- **Length** — arm length in pixels (up to 128).
- **Intensity** — strength; above 1 the arms and their source blow out.

## Use it when

- Night exteriors, concert lights, chandeliers or sun on water: point
  sources that should sparkle rather than merely glow.
- A glamour, jewellery or product shot that wants the cross-screen filter
  look on the speculars.
- A title or logo reveal where a single flare should sweep through the
  letters; animate the length or the intensity.

## Avoid when

- The highlights are large soft areas (sky, a white wall): they throw a
  smear of arms instead of a point of light; raise the threshold or use
  Bloom.
- Naturalistic drama. A star filter dates a shot instantly; one glint on
  one practical is already a lot.
- The wanted glint is tiny and crisp rather than long-armed: Glint is the
  small sparkle.
