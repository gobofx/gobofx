# Black Hole

Everything inside the radius falls towards a point: the picture slides
inward and twists as it goes, and a black core opens where it disappears.
The rim of the radius has no seam. At Amount 0 the picture passes through
unchanged. Pixels pulled from outside the frame repeat the edge.

- **Center** — where the picture falls in.
- **Radius** — how far the pull reaches, in frame heights.
- **Amount** — 0 is a no-op, 1 a full collapse with a wide core.

## Use it when

- A vanish or a swallow beat: keyframe Amount 0 → 1 and the shot eats
  itself, then cut.
- A portal, a drain, a singularity in a composite, centred on the prop.
- A music or a horror cut where the frame should feel pulled off its feet,
  held at a low amount for a few frames.

## Avoid when

- You want the picture to leave the frame in a direction: use a wipe or
  Spin, this one only goes down the hole.
- A face is inside the radius at anything but a brief peak.
- The core must stay see-through: the centre goes black, by design. Use
  Twirl for a swirl that keeps every pixel.
