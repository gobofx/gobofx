# Zoom & Pan

A push with speed on it: `to` shoves `from` out of frame while the picture
streaks radially and along the move, sharp at both ends and wildest at the
cut. The whip-pan of blur transitions.

- **Direction** — the way the pair travels.
- **Zoom** — radial streak at the midpoint, 0 off, 1 the full frame.
- **Blur** — smear along the move at the midpoint, 0 off, 1 the full frame.
- **Ease** — timing.

## Use it when

- Action, sport, travel: the cut should feel like the camera whipped to
  the next place.
- A beat-synced montage where every cut needs a kick, not a mix.
- Joining two handheld shots whose own motion already blurs.

## Avoid when

- Dialogue or anything quiet; the streak is loud and reads as a stunt.
- A static locked-off pair; a sudden rush between two still frames looks
  like a template.
- Text or logos cross the cut; they smear into unreadable bars.
