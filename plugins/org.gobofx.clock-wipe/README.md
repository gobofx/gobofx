# Clock Wipe

A radial edge sweeps around a point like a clock hand, revealing `to`.

- **Start Angle** — where the sweep begins (0 is straight up).
- **Clockwise** — direction of the sweep. **Center** — the pivot.
- **Softness** — feather of the edge; **Ease** — timing.

## Use it when

- Time passing, literally: a working day, a countdown, a deadline montage.
- Period flavour: newsreel, silent comedy, mid-century television.
- Comedy. The hand sweeping round is a wink to the audience.

## Avoid when

- Drama. The device is louder than the moment.
- Moving subjects near **Center**. The sweep slices them.
- You need a reveal from an edge rather than a pivot. Use Wipe or Inset
  Wipe. When in doubt, hard cut.
