# Simple

The plainest blur dissolve: the cross-fade itself goes soft, softest at the
cut, and both ends are sharp. One control, no streaks, no direction.

- **Radius** — the blur at the midpoint, in pixels.
- **Ease** — timing.

## Use it when

- A straight dissolve feels a touch hard and you want the join to melt
  rather than mix.
- Montage and music cuts where the pictures should blend without the
  cut becoming an event.
- Two shots with busy detail that moiré or strobe against each other in a
  plain cross-dissolve.

## Avoid when

- The cut needs direction or energy: Zoom & Pan or Blur Dissolve's
  streak carry motion, this one does not.
- Titles or graphics are in frame; soft text at the midpoint reads as a
  focus fault.
