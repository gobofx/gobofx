# Dots

A lattice of discs over a transparent background — halftone-like at small
sizes, a polka pattern at large ones.

- **Dot / Background** — colours with alpha.
- **Columns** — dots across the width; **Radius** — as a fraction of a cell.
- **Stagger** — hexagonal packing. **Softness** — feathers each disc.
- **Center** — a dot sits on the handle.

## Use it when

- A halftone or comic texture under titles, with **Background** transparent.
- A matte source: feed the dots to another effect and let the pattern drive
  it.
- A soft disc field as a graphic backdrop, with **Softness** up and a few
  columns.

## Avoid when

- Halftoning the picture itself. Use Halftone.
- As a picture on its own. It sits under something.
- Many small dots over moving video. They moiré on export and strobe on
  pans.
