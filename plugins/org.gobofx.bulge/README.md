# Bulge

Push the picture out as if over a sphere, or pull it in: positive Amount
bulges, negative pinches. The edge of the radius has no seam. At Amount 0 the
picture passes through unchanged. Pixels pulled from outside the frame repeat
the edge.

- **Center** — the top of the bulge.
- **Radius** — how far it reaches, in frame heights.
- **Amount** — -1 (full pinch) to 1 (full bulge); 0 is a no-op.

## Use it when

- A magnifying-glass or peephole beat: a small radius, 0.4–0.6 amount,
  keyframed on and off.
- A caricature or a comic push on a product, a logo or a face that is meant
  to be funny.
- Dressing a bubble, a lens or a drop of water in a composite, centred on it.

## Avoid when

- The picture needs a lens look across the whole frame: use Fisheye, which
  has a zoom to hide the edges.
- The subject is a face and the shot is not a joke; a bulge on skin reads as
  mockery at any amount.
- Text is inside the radius: the letters bend and stop being legible before
  the effect reads.
