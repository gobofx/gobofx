# Color Bars

SMPTE or EBU colour bars, or a stepped grey ramp. A known picture for
checking a monitor, a grade or an export chain.

- **Style** — smpte (bars, reverse bars, PLUGE), ebu (bars over black), ramp
  (16 grey steps over a continuous ramp).
- **Intensity** — bar level in sRGB; 0.75 is the broadcast standard.
- **Bottom Strip** — hide the lower strips for plain full-height bars.

## Use it when

- The head of a master or a leader, so whoever receives it can line up.
- Checking a monitor or a viewing room against a known picture.
- Checking an export chain. The ramp style shows banding and clipping that
  real footage hides.

## Avoid when

- As content in the picture, except as a dead-channel gag.
- Underneath grades or effects. It is a reference; the moment you touch it,
  it stops being one.
- As a texture or a colour bed. Use Stripes.
