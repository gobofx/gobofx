# Underwater

The wobble of looking through moving water: slow, crossing waves push the
picture about in both directions and drift with time, with a faint shimmer
of brightness riding on them like light through a surface. At Amount 0 the
picture passes through unchanged. Pixels pulled from outside the frame
repeat the edge.

- **Scale** — the size of the waves, in frame heights; small is a shimmer, large a slow heave.
- **Amount** — 0 is a no-op, 1 the full wobble.
- **Speed** — how fast the waves drift, in cycles per second; 0 freezes them, negative reverses them.

## Use it when

- A scene seen from under the surface, through a window of a tank, or
  reflected in a pool.
- A dream, a memory or a drunk point of view, kept small and slow so it
  reads as a feeling rather than a glitch.
- A heat haze over a road or an engine, with a small Scale and a high Speed.

## Avoid when

- The disturbance should come from one point, or ring outward: use Ripple
  or Droplet.
- Titles or thin lines are in frame; a wobble makes them swim unreadably.
- Footage already shot through real water; two wave patterns never agree.
