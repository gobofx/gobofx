# Solid

A single colour over the whole frame. Use it as a background under other
clips, or with a lower alpha as a tint layer over them.

- **Color** — the fill, with alpha. The default is a near-black.

## Use it when

- Black or white under everything, as a floor for alpha and fades.
- A tint layer at low alpha over a sequence, on the track.
- A colour card to fade through or hold on, and a placeholder for a missing
  shot.

## Avoid when

- Tinting skin through a low-alpha layer. Temperature in Color Adjustments
  does it without lifting the blacks.
- You want two colours or a fall-off. Use Gradient.
- Darkening the edges only. Use Vignette.
