# Scale

`from` shrinks to a point revealing `to`, or `to` grows from a point over
`from`, or both at once as a crossfade.

- **Mode** — shrink from, grow to, or both.
- **Center** — the point everything scales around.
- **Ease** — timing.

## Use it when

- A promo zoom-in reveal, **Center** on the subject in both shots.
- A photo arriving over footage with "grow to", and leaving with "shrink
  from".
- A graphic popping in on a beat.

## Avoid when

- Narrative. Hard cut.
- Faces off-centre. The scale drags them through the frame.
- You want streaks with the zoom. Use Zoom Dissolve. You want a turn, use
  Spin.
