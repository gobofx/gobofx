# Center

`to` opens outward from a point and grows until it fills the frame: as a
horizontal or vertical slit, a box with the frame's proportions, or a
circle. Move the point and the opening grows from there instead.

- **Shape** — horizontal slit, vertical slit, box or circle.
- **Center** — where the opening starts (the frame centre by default).
- **Softness** — feather of the edge; 0 is a hard line.
- **Ease** — timing.

## Use it when

- The new shot has a clear subject at its centre: the opening frames it.
- Classic and retro grammar, title reveals, iris-style openings (use circle).
- A matched pair with Inset Wipe: that grows from a corner, this from the middle.

## Avoid when

- Neither shot has a centre of interest; the opening then just looks like a hole.
- Fast cuts in busy footage; a soft dissolve is less distracting.
- You want the reveal to close rather than open; this always grows outward.
