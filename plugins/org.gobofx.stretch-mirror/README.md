# Stretch Mirror

A funhouse mirror: bands across the frame take turns stretching the middle
and squeezing the edges, then the reverse, along one axis. The frame's edges
stay pinned, so nothing is pulled in from outside it. At Amount 0 the
picture passes through unchanged.

- **Waves** — how many stretch-and-squeeze cycles fit across the frame.
- **Amount** — 0 is a no-op, 1 the strongest warp before the picture folds.
- **Axis** — horizontal stretches left and right with the bands stacked top
  to bottom (the tall mirror); vertical the other way round.

## Use it when

- The literal funhouse or hall-of-mirrors beat: a reflection in a fairground
  prop, a dream, a hangover.
- A caricature on a product or a mascot that is meant to be funny; one or
  two waves, half amount.
- A keyframed wobble on a title — Amount pulsing between 0 and 0.3 — that
  stays legible because the edges never move.

## Avoid when

- A wave travelling across the frame is what you mean: use Wave or Ripple,
  this one does not move on its own.
- A face in anything that is not a joke; the alternating bands make it
  read as a mask, not a mirror.
- Lines that must stay straight, or any shot with a horizon.
