# Plasma

The classic sum-of-sines plasma, mapped onto a rotating hue palette. Opaque;
stack an effect over it or use it as a background.

- **Scale** — waves across the frame; **Speed** — cycles per second.
- **Hue** — rotates the palette; **Saturation** — 0 is grey.
- **Complexity** — stretches the inner terms for busier patterns.

## Use it when

- Behind a title or a logo in a club, demo or retro-computing piece.
- A VJ bed under a performed effect.
- A texture to map or key through, with **Saturation** at 0.

## Avoid when

- As a picture on its own, or under dialogue of any kind.
- Corporate and documentary work. It dates the piece instantly.
- You want an organic texture. Use Noise.
