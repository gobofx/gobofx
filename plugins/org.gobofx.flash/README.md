# Flash

A cut hidden under a flash frame: the picture blows out to a colour at the
midpoint and settles on `to`.

- **Color** — the flash (white by default).
- **Width** — how much of the transition the flash occupies.
- **Ease** — timing.

## Use it when

- Hiding a cut on the beat in a music video or a trailer.
- A stylised flashback or a photography beat: the shutter fires, the
  picture changes.
- A diegetic flash: lightning, a camera, a muzzle.

## Avoid when

- Drama. A flash is loud by nature.
- Several in a row. Audiences hate it, and it is a photosensitivity risk.
- Hiding a bad cut. It draws the eye to the exact frame you wanted to hide.
  For a slower dip, use Fade To Color.
