# Sepia

A toned print: the picture goes monochrome, then its midtones go brown
while black stays black and white stays paper-white, the way a chemical
toner works. Amount 0 is neutral.

- **Amount** — 0 original, 1 full sepia.
- **Warmth** — how brown the midtones go; 0 is a plain black-and-white, 1
  is a deep chocolate tone.

## Use it when

- Archive, letter, photograph and flashback beats that must read as "old"
  in a single frame.
- Period title cards and inserts that sit beside real archive material and
  should not fight it.
- A whole act in the past, applied once on the track and kept at the same
  Warmth throughout.

## Avoid when

- Anything that should look merely warm rather than old: the Warm Sun
  preset on Color Adjustments keeps the colour.
- Partial Amount on colourful footage: half sepia is just a muddy warm
  desaturation; commit, or use Tint for a cast.
- The whites should take the tone too: this keeps them white on purpose;
  Tint or Colorize colour the whole range.
