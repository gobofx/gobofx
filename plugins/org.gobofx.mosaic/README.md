# Mosaic

The frame becomes a grid of tiles that flip over one by one, `from` on the
front and `to` on the back.

- **Columns / Rows** — the grid (Rows 0 = square tiles).
- **Stagger** — spread of the flips in time; **Order** — diagonal, random or row by row.
- **Ease** — timing.

## Use it when

- Photo galleries, title walls and promos, where the frame is a grid of
  pictures anyway.
- Kids' content and broadcast idents.
- A music cue that wants a cascade across the frame; set **Order** to
  diagonal and **Stagger** high.

## Avoid when

- Narrative. The tiles are a graphic device.
- Faces at low tile counts. Each tile turns through a feature.
- You want the flat version without 3D. Use Checker Wipe.
