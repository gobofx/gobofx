# Pinwheel

A multi-blade clock wipe: the frame is cut into equal sectors around the
centre and a blade sweeps across each one, revealing `to`, while the whole
wheel turns.

- **Blades** — how many sectors, 2 to 12.
- **Spin** — extra turns of the whole wheel over the transition, 0 for a
  plain sector wipe.
- **Softness** — feather of each blade's edge, as a fraction of its sector.
- **Ease** — timing.

## Use it when

- Retro, kids' and game-show looks where a geometric wipe is the point.
- Rounds, scores and segments: a wheel reads as "next turn".
- Countdowns and spinners, matching a clock or wheel motif on screen.

## Avoid when

- Drama and documentary; the sectors are a graphic device, never invisible.
- A single blade is what you want: that is Clock Wipe.
- Subjects near the centre, where every blade converges and the edge
  chatter is worst.
