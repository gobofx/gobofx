# Fisheye

A wide-angle lens: straight lines bow outward and the corners of the frame
pull in. What the lens cannot reach is transparent; raise **Zoom** to push the
picture out past the frame and hide it. At Amount 0 and Zoom 1 the picture
passes through unchanged.

- **Center** — the optical axis; move it to bend one side more than the other.
- **Amount** — 0 none, 1 a full barrel.
- **Zoom** — 1 shows the pulled-in edges, 1.3–1.6 usually hides them, 2 at the most.

## Use it when

- Action-camera, skate or helmet-cam energy on footage shot with a normal
  lens.
- A peephole, a door viewer or a security-camera point of view.
- A music or a comedy cut where the wide lens is the joke, held close to a
  face.

## Avoid when

- The job is to undo a real wide-lens shot; this adds barrel, it does not
  remove it.
- Architecture or anything with lines that must stay straight.
- Graphics and titles, unless they are made to look shot off a screen.
