# Puzzle

`from` breaks into jigsaw pieces that spin off, shrink and fade, each in its
own time, until `to` is uncovered. The pieces have real knobs and sockets;
the cut lines follow a grid you choose.

- **Columns** — pieces across; rows follow so the pieces are roughly square.
- **Scatter** — how far a piece flies before it is gone, in cells.
- **Stagger** — 0 every piece leaves together, 1 one after another.
- **Ease** — timing.

## Use it when

- Something is literally coming apart or being solved: puzzles, plans,
  team pieces, "it all falls into place" in reverse.
- Kids', education and craft content, where the jigsaw is the brand.
- A reveal that should feel playful rather than slick.

## Avoid when

- Drama, interviews, anything that should not call attention to the cut.
- Faces fill the frame; cutting a face into pieces reads as violence.
- Under a second; the pieces need time to be seen as pieces.
