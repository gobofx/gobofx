# Crystallize

The picture broken into irregular crystal cells: a Voronoi tiling where
every cell takes the average colour of the picture under it, with the seams
between cells darkened like facets. **Amount** 0 is the untouched source.

- **Cell Size** — average cell width in pixels; 12–20 px keeps a face, 40+ is stained glass.
- **Edge Darkness** — how dark the borders between cells are; 0 is a flat mosaic, 1 draws black leading.
- **Seed** — reshuffles the cell layout; animate it for a shimmering, re-crystallising surface.
- **Amount** — 0 is the source, 1 the full effect.

## Use it when

- A stained-glass or frosted-glass treatment on a title bed or a background plate.
- A transition beat: animate **Cell Size** up from 4 px and the picture shatters into facets, then back.
- A frozen, ice or gem motif in a graphics package, where the irregular cells carry the idea better than square pixels.

## Avoid when

- Anonymising a face or a plate; the cells follow the picture and leak its colour. Pixellate is the honest censor.
- Fast-moving footage with a fixed **Seed**: the cells stand still while the picture moves under them and reads as a dirty window.
- Small **Cell Size** on an entire scene: it reads as a codec fault, not a look.
