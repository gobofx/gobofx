# Bloom

A cross-dissolve whose highlights bloom: the bright parts of both shots
swell into a glow that peaks at the cut and is gone by either end. The
picture never blows out; it glows.

- **Intensity** — bloom strength at the midpoint; above 1 is hot.
- **Radius** — how far the glow spreads, in pixels.
- **Tint** — the glow's colour (white by default).
- **Ease** — timing.

## Use it when

- Romantic, dreamy or memory sequences: the glow softens the join and
  flatters skin.
- Shots with real light sources (windows, lamps, sun) that should flare
  across the cut.
- A gentler alternative to Flash when a white-out is too much.

## Avoid when

- Dark, low-key material with no highlights: there is nothing to bloom,
  so it is just a dissolve.
- Documentary or news; the glow reads as styling.
- Graphics with thin white lines in frame; they haze into blobs.
