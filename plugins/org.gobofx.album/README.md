# Album

The frame is an open photo album with its spine down the middle. One page
turns over like a leaf of a book, `from` on its face and `to` on its back,
revealing `to` beneath; a soft shadow runs ahead of the turning page and
the gutter darkens while it is lifted.

- **Direction** — the right page turns to the left, or the left page to the right.
- **Perspective** — how far the lifted page edge comes toward the viewer.
- **Shadow** — strength of the page shadow and the gutter shade; 0 is a flat turn.
- **Ease** — timing.

## Use it when

- Photo slideshows, wedding and family films, memorial and anniversary pieces.
- Chaptered storytelling where each shot is literally the next page.
- Stills and graphic cards, which read as pages better than moving footage.

## Avoid when

- Fast cutting; a page needs a second or more to turn convincingly.
- Modern, hard-edged or documentary material: the album is sentimental by design.
- The subject sits on the centre line; the spine and the shadow cross it.
