# Indent

Press the picture into a surface. Brightness is read as depth, a light from
**Light Angle** shades the slopes, and the shading sits over the original
colour, like an inner bevel stamped into leather or card. **Amount** 0
returns the source untouched.

- **Light Angle** — in degrees; 0 lights from the right, 90 from above, 135 from the upper left.
- **Depth** — in pixels; how far the surface is pressed, which sets both the width and the strength of the bevel. 0 is flat.
- **Amount** — 0 is the source, 1 is the full press.

## Use it when

- A logo, a title or a cut-out should look stamped or debossed into the backing: a small **Depth** (2–4 px) with a light from the top left.
- A flat graphic wants a tactile, embossed-card feel without losing its colours.
- A texture layer (paper, leather, stone) needs a little physical relief before it is blended under something else.

## Avoid when

- You want the raised, grey, 1990s emboss: that is Relief.
- Live-action with faces: skin pores and hair become ridges and it reads as a filter, not a finish.
- The footage is noisy; every grain becomes a dent. Blur a little first or keep **Depth** at 1.
