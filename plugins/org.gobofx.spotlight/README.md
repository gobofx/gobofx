# Spotlight

A soft pool of light over transparent, like a single lamp aimed at the
frame. Stack it over a clip to lift one area, or under titles as a glow
plate.

- **Center** — where the lamp points.
- **Radius** — pool radius in frame heights.
- **Aspect** — width over height; above 1 stretches the pool sideways, like a
  raked light.
- **Softness** — how much of the radius is fade; 0 is a hard-edged follow spot.
- **Color** — the light, with alpha.
- **Intensity** — 0 is off; above 1 widens the saturated core.

## Use it when

- A frame needs one obvious point of attention and the shot has none: a face
  in a wide, a product on a table.
- Titles sit on a flat background and want a lit plate behind them, not a
  drop shadow.
- You want a stage or interrogation-room feel built from several lamps on
  separate layers.

## Avoid when

- The job is darkening the edges rather than lighting the middle: use
  Vignette, which keeps the centre untouched.
- Daylight exteriors: a pool of light on grass reads as a mistake, not a mood.
- The light should move with the subject and you cannot keyframe Center.
