# Cross Dissolve

The default transition: `from` fades out as `to` fades in.

- **Ease** — 0 is a linear blend; 1 starts and ends gently (smoothstep).

## Use it when

- Time passes. The only transition that says it without comment.
- The end of a scene into the next location, when a cut would feel abrupt
  and a fade to black too final.
- Montage, where shots share a subject or a mood and should flow.

## Avoid when

- Inside a conversation. Dialogue cuts hard; a dissolve makes every line a
  memory.
- Two shots full of moving detail. The overlap turns to mud.
- Hiding a jump cut. It advertises the jump; a cutaway or a hard cut
  does the job. For black between scenes, use Fade To Color.
