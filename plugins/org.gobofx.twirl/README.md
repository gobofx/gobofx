# Twirl

Swirl the picture around a point; the twist is strongest at the centre and
fades out at the radius.

- **Center**, **Radius** (in frame heights), **Angle** at the centre.

## Use it when

- A single stylised beat: drunk, drugged, dizzy, a flashback door.
- A graphic or a title spinning into itself; animate **Angle**.
- A vortex behind a key, applied to the backing layer only.

## Avoid when

- Faces inside **Radius**. The twist pulls through them.
- The whole project. One stylisation per project, and this is not it.
- You want it as a cut. Use Spin or Ripple. For a travelling wave, use Wave.
