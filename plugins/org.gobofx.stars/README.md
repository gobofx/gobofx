# Stars

Five-point stars sweep across the cut from left to right. Each star swells
over its own patch of the frame, the patch beneath it becomes `to` while it
is covered, and the star shrinks away to reveal it. The stars are drawn
procedurally, so they stay crisp at any size.

- **Count** — stars in the field (1–24).
- **Size** — star scale; 1 lets each star cover its patch, less leaves gaps that dissolve late.
- **Color** — the star fill (alpha respected).
- **Ease** — timing.

## Use it when

- A celebratory or playful beat: kids' content, party recaps, award moments.
- A sting between segments of a show where a graphic punctuation is the point.
- You want a colour hit on the cut; set the colour to a brand accent.

## Avoid when

- The footage is documentary, dramatic or interview material: the stars read
  as a joke.
- Both shots are busy; the swelling shapes add clutter instead of punctuation.
  Use a plain dissolve or a wipe.
