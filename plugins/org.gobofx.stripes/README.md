# Stripes

Parallel bands of two colours at any angle, with adjustable duty cycle and
an optional scroll.

- **Count** — stripe pairs across the frame height; **Angle** — direction.
- **Duty** — how much of each pair is Color A.
- **Softness** — blurs the edges; **Speed** — scrolls the pattern.

## Use it when

- Hazard or caution bars behind a warning card.
- A retro or sports graphic bed, with **Speed** on for a slow scroll.
- A matte source for a striped wipe edge or a shutter.

## Avoid when

- As a picture on its own.
- Thin stripes in motion. They moiré on export and strobe on screen; keep
  **Count** low and **Softness** up.
- Over footage with its own lines: blinds, railings, stairs.
