# Multi-Flip

A grid of tiles, each a small card with `from` on the front and `to` on the
back, turns over in a wave that spreads out from a point you choose. The
area a turning tile does not cover is transparent.

- **Columns / Rows** — the grid (Rows 0 = square tiles).
- **Axis** — tiles turn about their vertical or horizontal axis, or alternate.
- **Origin** — where the wave starts (centre by default).
- **Stagger** — 0 every tile turns together, 1 a slow wave across the frame.
- **Perspective** — foreshortening of each turning tile.
- **Ease** — timing.

## Use it when

- A reveal should ripple out from a subject: put Origin on it.
- Infographic, tech and product material where a tiled card motif fits.
- You need a flip with more texture than a single card; Flip turns the whole frame.

## Avoid when

- Drama and documentary; it is a graphic device and reads as one.
- Fine detail matters mid-cut: the tiles cut faces and text at their edges.
- The order of the tiles is the point, not their origin: Mosaic offers diagonal,
  random and row orders.
