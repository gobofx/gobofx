# Widescreen

Letterbox or pillarbox bars that frame the picture at a chosen aspect ratio.

- **Aspect** — a preset, or **Custom Ratio**.
- **Offset** — slide the visible window up/down (or left/right).
- **Color** — the bars; a transparent colour crops instead of painting bars.
- **Feather** — soften the bar edges, in pixels.

## Use it when

- Delivering at 2.39 or 1.85, applied once on the track so every shot shares
  the frame.
- Cropping a 16:9 to square or vertical with a transparent **Color**.
- Re-framing a shot inside the bars with **Offset**.

## Avoid when

- Letterboxing some shots and not others. The bars jump at every cut.
- Footage already letterboxed. You get bars inside bars.
- Any **Feather**. Cinema bars are hard; a soft bar reads as a mistake. For a
  shaped crop, use Shape Mask.
