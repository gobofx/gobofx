# Glow

The bright parts of the picture bloom outward.

- **Threshold** — brightness where the glow starts; **Softness** — the knee.
- **Radius** — spread in pixels. **Intensity** — strength (above 1 is hot).

## Use it when

- Lifting a title or a logo off its background.
- Practicals: neon, candles, screens, headlamps, where the sensor clipped
  what the eye saw bloom.
- A dream or beauty pass at low **Intensity** and a high **Threshold**.

## Avoid when

- Faces at any real intensity. Skin blows out before the lights do.
- Footage already clipped in the highlights. There is nothing left to glow.
- As a stylisation on every shot. One stylisation per project; if the whole
  show needs a halo, pick Bloom and leave it alone.
