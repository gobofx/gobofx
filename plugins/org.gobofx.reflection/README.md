# Reflection

The frame pulls back to a card standing on a reflective floor. `from` rises
out of the top while its reflection fades, `to` rises in from below with its
own reflection and grows back to fill the frame. The floor itself is
transparent, so the background beneath shows through.

- **Size** — the card's scale while it stands on the floor.
- **Reflection** — strength of the mirrored copy; 0 is a plain lift.
- **Ease** — timing.

## Use it when

- Product, architecture and showroom material: the floor sells polish.
- Presentations and portfolio reels, where each clip is an exhibit.
- The cut sits over a dark or graphic background that the floor can show.

## Avoid when

- Narrative or documentary footage; the card pull-back is pure presentation.
- Handheld or shaky shots: the reflection doubles every jitter.
- The background beneath is another video: the floor shows it, and the
  reflections fight it.
