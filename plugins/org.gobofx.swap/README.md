# Swap

Two cards trade places in depth: `from` swings aside and shrinks into the
background while `to` swings out from behind it and grows to fill the frame.
They pass each other at the midpoint; the gap around them is transparent.

- **Direction** — the side `from` swings to; `to` takes the other.
- **Depth** — how small the card at the back gets, 0 flat, 1 to nothing.
- **Ease** — timing.

## Use it when

- Comparisons and before/after pairs, where "these two trade" is the message.
- Card-based interfaces, decks and app walkthroughs.
- A playful two-shot swap in a music or lifestyle edit.

## Avoid when

- Full-frame live action with nothing behind it; the gap shows the void.
- A cut that should be invisible; this one is a little animation.
- Both shots are busy edge to edge: two shrunken frames side by side is a
  lot to read at once.
