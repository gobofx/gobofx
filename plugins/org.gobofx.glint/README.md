# Glint

Specular glints: a small, crisp four-ray sparkle on the brightest spots,
the ping a sun on chrome or an eye-light gives. Short rays with a fast
falloff keep it a pip of light rather than a flare. Intensity 0 is a no-op.

- **Threshold** — brightness that sparkles; keep it high so only the speculars do.
- **Size** — ray length in pixels (1 to 48).
- **Intensity** — strength; above 1 the pips blow out.
- **Angle** — rotation of the cross in degrees; 45 is an X, 0 a plus.

## Use it when

- Jewellery, glassware, chrome and water: the speculars should wink.
- Eye-lights and teeth in a beauty or fashion piece, at a size of a few
  pixels.
- Animated sparkle on a logo or a magic moment: keyframe the intensity and
  it twinkles.

## Avoid when

- Broad highlights (sky, windows, a white shirt) sit above the threshold:
  they grow a fringe of rays and the sparkle is lost; raise the threshold.
- Long dramatic arms are wanted: that is Dazzle, with its point count and
  length.
- Anything that should look like it was lit rather than filtered; a glint is
  an ornament.
