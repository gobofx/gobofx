# Inset Wipe

A box of `to` grows from a corner, or outward from the centre, until it
fills the frame.

- **Corner** — the box origin (or center).
- **Softness** — feather of the box edge; **Ease** — timing.

## Use it when

- A picture-in-picture arriving: a map, an insert, a second camera.
- Presentational and broadcast cuts between cards and charts.
- A corner reveal that mirrors where the new content will live.

## Avoid when

- Narrative. A growing box is an interface, not a cut.
- The centre option over faces. The box edge passes through the eyes.
- You want the two shots to blend. That is Cross Dissolve.
