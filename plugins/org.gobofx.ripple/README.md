# Ripple

Concentric rings spread from a point and distort the picture while `from`
dissolves into `to`.

- **Center** — where the drop lands; **Rings** — ring density.
- **Amplitude** — distortion strength; **Speed** — how far the rings travel.
- **Ease** — timing.

## Use it when

- A dream, memory or water entrance, where the surface breaking is the point.
- A fantasy reveal or a title arriving out of still water.
- A single, placed moment, with **Center** on the thing that triggers it.

## Avoid when

- Narrative flashbacks. It is the 1980s sitcom device and the audience
  knows it.
- Faces near **Center**. The rings warp through them.
- You want the distortion without the cut. Use the Ripple distort effect,
  or Wave for a travelling sine.
