# Spin Slide

`from` spins as it slides out one side while `to` spins in from the other,
both turning the same way and arriving square. The frame between them is
transparent while they pass.

- **Direction** — the way the pair travels.
- **Turns** — rotations each frame makes on its way, 0.25 to 2.
- **Ease** — timing.

## Use it when

- Energetic, graphic edits: promos, sports packages, bumpers.
- Panels and cards over a background that can show through the gap.
- Matching a spinning motif on screen (wheels, records, logos).

## Avoid when

- There is nothing behind the clip: the transparent gap becomes a black
  hole in the middle of the cut.
- Quiet or long-form content; it is a stunt, and it looks like one.
- More than one turn on a full-frame shot: at speed the picture reads as
  a blur, not a spin.
