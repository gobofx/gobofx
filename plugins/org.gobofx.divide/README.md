# Divide

`from` breaks into strips that slide off-frame in alternating directions,
one after another, revealing `to`.

- **Strips** — how many; **Orientation** — horizontal strips slide
  sideways, vertical strips slide up and down.
- **Stagger** — 0 moves every strip at once, 1 staggers them fully.
- **Ease** — timing.

## Use it when

- Broadcast promos, sports packages and title sequences, where the frame is
  allowed to break.
- Cuts between graphic cards on the downbeat.
- A music video section that wants energy without a 3D move.

## Avoid when

- Narrative. The strips are a graphic device.
- Two live-action shots with faces. The strips slice through them.
- You want the simpler movement. Push and Slide do it with less noise.
