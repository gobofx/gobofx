# Zoom Blur

Radial streaks from a point, the push-in look.

- **Center** — the point the streaks radiate from. **Amount** — streak length.

## Use it when

- A push-in hit in a trailer or a music video, **Center** on the subject.
- Speed and impact frames, two or three at a time.
- A point-of-view beat: a blow, a rush, a fall.

## Avoid when

- Faces for longer than a beat. The streaks pull through the eyes.
- A stylisation over a whole scene.
- You want it as a cut. Use Zoom Dissolve. Along a line, use Directional
  Blur.
