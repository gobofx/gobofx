# Fade To Color

`from` fades to a solid colour, holds, then `to` fades in from it. The
classic dip-to-black; set the colour to white for a dip-to-white.

- **Color** — the dip colour (alpha is respected).
- **Hold** — how long the colour is held, as a fraction of the transition.
- **Ease** — timing of the two fades.

## Use it when

- The end of a scene or an act break. Black means stop; white means light,
  memory or heaven.
- A long passage of time, longer than a dissolve admits.
- A promo that dips through a brand colour between beats.

## Avoid when

- Inside a scene. A fade is punctuation at the end of a thought, not in the
  middle of one.
- Too often. Five fades to black and the film feels like episodes.
- A dip to white on a bright shot. It reads as an exposure fault; Flash is
  built for that hit. The default remains the hard cut.
