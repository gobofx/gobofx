# Sphere

Wrap the picture under a disc onto a ball: the centre magnifies, the rim
compresses, and a soft shading darkens the limb so the curve reads as a
solid. Outside the ball the frame is transparent. At Amount 0 the picture
passes through unchanged; nothing is pulled from outside the frame.

- **Center** — where the ball sits.
- **Radius** — the ball's radius, in frame heights.
- **Amount** — 0 is a no-op, 1 a full ball; the outside fades out with it.

## Use it when

- A globe, a marble, a crystal ball or a planet built from a map, a texture
  or a still, dropped over another clip.
- A logo or a badge that should turn into a button or a bauble, keyframing
  Amount from 0 so the ball rises out of the flat graphic.
- A lens or a bubble element in a composite, with the ball's own shot
  showing through it.

## Avoid when

- The frame should stay full: use Bulge, which keeps the picture around the
  bump.
- A fisheye or a wide-lens look is wanted; a sphere has an edge and a
  shadow, a lens does not.
- The picture has to spin: this wraps one still view, it does not rotate the
  texture around the ball.
