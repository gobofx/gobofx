# Flop

Mirror the picture.

- **Mode** — horizontal (the usual flop), vertical, or both (a 180° turn).

## Use it when

- Fixing screen direction when a shot crosses the line and there is no
  other take.
- A graphic built the wrong way round.
- A reflection in a composite: flop the layer, lower its opacity.

## Avoid when

- Readable text, logos, clocks or steering wheels in frame. The audience
  finds them.
- A known face. People are not symmetrical, and viewers notice on anyone
  they recognise.
- You want a mirrored half-frame, not a flopped whole. Use Mirror.
