# Hue/Saturation

Rotate every hue around the colour wheel, then scale saturation and brightness.

- **Hue** — degrees of rotation (180 swaps complementary colours).
- **Saturation** — 0 is monochrome. **Value** — brightness scale.

## Use it when

- Pulling a shot to monochrome, or pulling a graphic back so it stops
  fighting the footage.
- Nudging a costume, prop or wall colour, through a mask on its own layer.
- A quick **Value** trim on a graphic layer.

## Avoid when

- Any **Hue** on a shot with skin in it. Ten degrees makes a face sick;
  thirty makes it a corpse. Mask the subject out first.
- As the grade. Exposure, contrast and warmth live in Color Adjustments.
- Correcting a white balance error. Temperature and tint in Color
  Adjustments do that honestly; a hue rotation shifts everything.
