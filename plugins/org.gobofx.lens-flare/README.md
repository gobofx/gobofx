# Lens Flare

A light source with its glow, an anamorphic streak and a chain of ghosts
reflected through the frame centre, over transparent. Move the light and
the ghosts slide along the axis the way a real lens does.

- **Light** — where the source is; drag it onto the sun or the lamp.
- **Intensity** — 0 off, 1 full; above 1 blows the core out.
- **Ghosts** — how many reflections, 0 to 12.
- **Color** — tint of the source and its glow.
- **Streak** — horizontal streak length in frame heights; 0 for a clean
  spherical look.
- **Size** — glow radius in frame heights; the ghosts scale with it.

## Use it when

- The sun or a practical lamp is in frame and the lens did not flare: place
  the light on it and keyframe the move.
- A stylised, anamorphic look: long Streak, cool tint, intensity under 1 so
  the plate still reads.
- A hit, a reveal or a transition beat needs a flash that has a source.

## Avoid when

- Nothing in frame is bright enough to flare; a flare with no source is the
  most tired effect in the toolbox.
- The light goes behind an object and the flare should cut; it does not
  occlude, mask it or avoid it.
- Dialogue close-ups: a ghost drifting across a face pulls focus every time.
