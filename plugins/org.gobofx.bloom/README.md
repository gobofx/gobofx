# Bloom

The highlights bleed into their surroundings, the way bright light fogs a
lens: a soft-kneed bright pass, a wide blur, and the haze added back with a
colour of its own. Intensity 0 is a no-op.

- **Threshold** — brightness where the bleed starts; lower it to fog the mids too.
- **Radius** — spread in pixels (up to 64); the haze is about twice as wide as Glow at the same value.
- **Intensity** — strength of the haze; above 1 the brights blow out.
- **Tint** — the haze's colour; white keeps the source hue, a warm tint reads as sun.

## Use it when

- A sunlit exterior, a window or practical lights should fog softly into the
  frame the way a vintage lens does.
- Neon, screens or a night exterior need their brights to breathe without
  sharpening the picture.
- A dream, memory or flashback needs a gentle lift and a tinted haze
  rather than a hard glow.

## Avoid when

- The brights should stay crisp with a tight halo: Glow has the knee and
  radius for that.
- The whole frame is bright: everything blooms into milk; raise the
  threshold or pick a different look.
- Skin fills the frame: a bloom on faces reads as a soft-focus filter from
  another decade unless the threshold sits above the skin tones.
