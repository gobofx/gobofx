# Target

Cut the picture into concentric rings around a point and turn each ring by
its own angle, so the frame breaks into a dial of misaligned bands. At
Rotation 0 the picture passes through unchanged. Where a turned ring runs
out of picture it is transparent.

- **Center** — the middle of the rings.
- **Rings** — how many, 2 to 24, from the centre to the farthest corner.
- **Rotation** — degrees per ring; negative turns the other way.
- **Alternate** — on, every other ring turns the opposite way; off, each ring turns one step further than the one inside it.

## Use it when

- A dial, a lock or a safe-cracking beat: keyframe Rotation to 0 so the
  rings click into place.
- A hypnotic or a vertigo cue over a face or an eye, with the centre on the
  pupil and a small rotation.
- A graphic treatment for a logo or a record sleeve, with Alternate off for a
  spiral twist.

## Avoid when

- A smooth swirl is wanted: use Twirl, which has no seams between the
  bands.
- Text crosses the rings; every ring boundary cuts the letters.
- The centre is near a frame edge; the outer rings then pull mostly from
  outside the picture and the frame fills with transparency.
