# Band Wipe

The frame splits into bands that each wipe to `to`, neighbouring bands
travelling in opposite directions.

- **Bands** — how many; **Direction** — travel of the even bands.
- **Alternate** — odd bands go the other way.
- **Softness** — feather of the edges; **Ease** — timing.

## Use it when

- A period or comedy cut. Alternating bands read as 1970s television or a
  game show, and that is the joke.
- A montage of stills or title cards, where the picture is flat and the wipe
  is the event.
- Cuts on the beat in a promo. Set **Bands** to the bar count and let the
  alternation carry the rhythm.

## Avoid when

- Drama. The bands shout; a hard cut or a Cross Dissolve carries the scene.
- Two moving shots. Opposing bands over motion read as a playback glitch,
  not a wipe. Use Wipe.
- More than once per project, unless the show is built on it.
