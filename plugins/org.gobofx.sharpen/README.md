# Sharpen

Unsharp-mask sharpening: the picture minus a blurred copy, added back.

- **Amount** — strength. **Radius** — the scale of detail that is enhanced.

## Use it when

- A slightly soft shot that otherwise matches, at a light **Amount**.
- Graphics or text that were scaled up.
- Matching a softer camera to a sharper one in a multi-camera cut.

## Avoid when

- Noisy or grainy footage. You sharpen the grain first.
- Skin above **Amount** 1. Pores and halos arrive before detail does.
- As a track-wide default. For flat areas that must stay quiet, use Unsharp
  Mask and its threshold. Nothing fixes missed focus.
