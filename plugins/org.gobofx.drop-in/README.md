# Drop In

`to` falls into frame from the top, lands on `from` with a small bounce and
settles. A soft shadow runs ahead of its edge so it reads as a card landing,
not a wipe.

- **Bounce** — 0 lands softly, 1 bounces hard.
- **Shadow** — darkness of the shadow `to` throws on `from` ahead of its edge.
- **Ease** — timing of the fall itself.

## Use it when

- Cards, slates, title plates or captions arriving over a scene.
- Playful or upbeat cuts where a bit of physics sells the arrival.
- Social and explainer edits that stack shots like panels.

## Avoid when

- Narrative scene changes; the bounce is a gag, and it plays every time.
- Two full-frame live-action shots with nothing card-like about them.
- Slow transitions: a long fall with a bounce at the end feels broken.
