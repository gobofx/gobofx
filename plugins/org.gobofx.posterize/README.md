# Posterize

Reduce the picture to a handful of tonal steps, for a screen-print look.

- **Levels** — steps per channel.
- **Gamma** — the space the steps are spaced in; 2.2 looks even on a display.
- **Per Channel** — quantise each channel (colourful bands) or brightness only.

## Use it when

- A screen-print or poster graphic made from a still.
- A bold flat look for a title bed or a music video section, chosen once
  for the project.
- Pushing a matte to hard steps before it drives another effect.

## Avoid when

- Faces at low **Levels**. Skin becomes bands of orange and brown.
- Skies and gradients. The banding is the whole picture.
- A limited-palette look that still has to flatter people. Use LUT.
